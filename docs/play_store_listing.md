## Full description (max 4000 characters)

UniShram is India's unified platform connecting daily-wage labourers, contractors, clients, and material vendors — all in one simple app.

**For Labourers**
Find daily-wage work near you. See jobs posted by contractors in your area, apply in one tap, and get notified the moment you're accepted. Set your skills and preferred work radius so you only see relevant jobs.

**For Contractors**
Post jobs, find reliable workers nearby, and manage applications easily. Estimate costs and build your team based on skill and location.

**For Clients**
Looking for a contractor or worker for your project? Post your requirement and get matched with verified professionals near your project location.

**For Vendors**
List your construction materials and reach contractors actively looking for supplies in your area.

**Why UniShram?**
- Built for real daily-wage workers — simple, voice-guided, multilingual interface (supports 20+ Indian languages)
- Location-based matching — find work or workers within your preferred radius
- Secure phone number sign-in — no complicated passwords
- Ratings and reviews — build trust with every completed job
- Report and block — a safety system to keep the community respectful
- Fair minimum wage guidance built in, aligned with government wage standards

UniShram is built to serve India's informal labour economy with dignity, transparency, and simplicity — because finding honest work, or an honest worker, shouldn't be hard.
