# Privacy Policy for UniShram

**Last updated:** [DATE — fill in when you publish]

UniShram ("we", "our", "the app") connects daily-wage labourers, contractors, clients, and material vendors across India. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it is handled.

## Information We Collect

- **Mobile number** — used to sign you in via SMS verification (OTP). We do not use this for marketing.
- **Name, gender, languages spoken** — shown on your profile so other users can identify and communicate with you.
- **Location (state, city/town, PIN code, and precise GPS location if you choose "Use my current location")** — used to match you with nearby jobs or workers. Contractors and clients may set a search radius; this uses your location to find people/jobs within that radius.
- **Profile photo and work photos** — optional, uploaded by you, shown to other users to help them recognise or evaluate you.
- **Job postings, applications, and chat messages** — the core functionality of the app; visible to the other party in a given job/application/chat.
- **Ratings and reviews** — left by other users after a job, visible on your public profile.
- **Reports** — if you report another user, we record the report to review for safety/moderation purposes.
- **Device push notification token** — used to send you notifications (e.g. "Your application was accepted"). We do not use this for advertising.
- **Usage analytics** — anonymised app usage data (e.g. which screens are used) via Firebase Analytics, to help us improve the app. This is not linked to your name or phone number.
- **Crash reports** — if the app crashes, technical diagnostic information is sent automatically via Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. This does not include your personal messages or photos.

## How We Use Your Information

- To create and operate your account.
- To match you with relevant jobs, workers, or contractors based on location and skills.
- To enable communication (chat) between users involved in a job.
- To display your profile, ratings, and reviews to other users as intended by the app's core function.
- To enforce our moderation policy (reviewing reports, suspending accounts that violate community standards).
- To improve the app through anonymised analytics and crash reporting.

## Who Can See Your Information

- Other users can see your profile (name, photo, ratings, location area) as part of normal marketplace browsing.
- Your exact GPS coordinates are never shown to other users directly — only used internally to calculate distance/matching.
- We do not sell your personal information to third parties.
- We do not share your data with advertisers.
